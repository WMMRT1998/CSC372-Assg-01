Community Spotlight: Cape Fear Botanical Garden Mocktail Party

Experience a day of relaxation and community at the Cape Fear Botanical Garden Mocktail Party.
Here you will be able to relax, and drink mocktails in the beautiful Cape Fear Botanical Garden.
This event is perfect for those looking to unwind and enjoy the beauty of nature while sipping on delicious non-alcoholic beverages.

Event Activities

  1. Pure Barre Class
    • Join us for a Pure Barre class led by a certified instructor.
      This low-impact workout is perfect for all fitness levels and
      will leave you feeling energized and refreshed.
  2. Handcrafted Mocktails
  3. Local Artisans
    • The Flower Crystal Nymph
    • The Drip Bar
    • Infinite Bliss Healing
    • Pure Barre
    • Alta Sol
    • And more!
  4. Food Trucks
    • xQuisito International
    • Once Upon a Bite
    • Etheridge Charcuterie
    • Lucia's Coffee & Treats
  5. Live Music
    • Enjoy live music from Matt from Boston

Attendee Tips

  • General Admission is $25, but $15 if you are a member at Garden
  • Wear comfortable shoes, so that you can enjoy the event without discomfort
  • Walk in ticket purchase is available at the door
  • Parking is free, and at a first come first served basis. So arrive early!
